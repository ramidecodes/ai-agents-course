About the course

From isolated conversations to a durable workspace

Learners turn scattered business knowledge and isolated AI conversations into a persistent, practical workspace. They create an inspectable memory system, organize approved business context, add reusable skills, and run recurring workflows for strategy, brand, content, and execution.

Builds on AI Agents Fundamentals. Theory is paired with guided setup—for entrepreneurs, creators, coaches, consultants, and small teams who remain responsible for strategic choices, facts, permissions, and consequential actions.

What you’ll leave with

A workspace that remembers what you approve

By the end, learners can build and maintain a persistent AI workspace that supports recurring strategy, brand, content, and execution workflows—without handing the business over to the agent.

  1. Set up and verify a persistent AI workspace with inspectable canonical sources and a replaceable retrieval or memory layer.
  2. Design a useful business ontology and manage the lifecycle of private, project, and shared knowledge.
  3. Create an approved business, audience, offer, brand, and content source of truth grounded in evidence.
  4. Build reusable skills and supervised workflows for planning, research, decisions, content, and recurring reviews.
  5. Complete one recurring business workflow and maintain its context through capture, confirmation, retrieval, correction, and archiving.

For entrepreneurs, creators, coaches, independent professionals, and small teams who have completed AI Agents Fundamentals or have equivalent experience. Not a course on agent engineering, custom model training, or fully autonomous business operations.

Scope

Controlled persistence, human ownership

The goal is not an AI system that remembers everything or runs the business unattended. It is a controlled workspace that retrieves the right context, preserves confirmed knowledge, supports useful work, and remains understandable to its owner.

Keep business truth inspectable, memory curated, skills testable, and consequential decisions human-owned.
